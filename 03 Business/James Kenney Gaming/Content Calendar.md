## Weekly production calendar

The available budget is approximately 18 hours per week.

| Day | Time | Focus |
|---|---:|---|
| Monday | 2 hr | Finalize Tuesday upload; prep Thursday/Saturday overlays |
| Tuesday | 2 hr | Publish at 3 PM Central; monitor checks/comments; record or edit next episode |
| Wednesday | 2 hr | Finalize Thursday upload; continue Saturday edit |
| Thursday | 2 hr | Publish at 3 PM Central; monitor checks/comments; record or edit next episode |
| Friday | 2 hr | Finalize Saturday upload; build next-week buffer |
| Saturday | 4 hr | Publish at 3 PM Central; quality review; batch chapters/thumbnails |
| Sunday | 4 hr | Analytics review; route next episodes; record buffer; optional Short only if strong |

If the episode is already complete, use the extra time to build a buffer rather than adding unnecessary polish. If the buffer drops below one finished episode, pause Shorts first before reducing long-form quality.
