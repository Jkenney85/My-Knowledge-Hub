# Episode 11 — Epona and the Hookshot

## Viewer promise

- **Episode goal:** Begin adult Link’s route by getting Epona, racing Dampé for the Hookshot, and collecting the first adult-era Heart Pieces.
- **Why it matters to 100%:** Epona is needed for the Big Poe bottle quest, the Hookshot unlocks Forest Temple access and adult collectibles, and Heart Pieces #18–19 move the heart count forward.
- **Expected runtime:** Approximately 50–80 minutes; do not exceed 90 minutes.
- **Next episode tease:** Use the Hookshot for Kakariko/Castle Skulltulas, Dampe’s timed Heart Piece, and the road back to the forest.

## Route

### 7.1 — Getting Epona

- [ ] Exit Temple of Time and cross ruined Castle Town
- [ ] Enter the former guardhouse / Ghost Shop and note it for later
- [ ] Travel to Lon Lon Ranch
- [ ] Pay Ingo for a corral ride
- [ ] Play Epona’s Song and practice riding Epona
- [ ] Pay Ingo again and win the first race
- [ ] Accept and win the second race
- [ ] Escape Lon Lon Ranch by jumping the outer fence/wall
- [ ] Optional: complete Malon’s obstacle course under 50 seconds to earn the cow at Link’s house

### 7.2 — Getting the Hookshot

- [ ] Travel to Kakariko Village
- [ ] Enter the Graveyard
- [ ] Ride the Magic Bean plant on the left side of the Graveyard
- [ ] Break the crate on the upper ledge
- [ ] Collect Heart Piece #18
- [ ] Pull back the flower-marked grave near the Magic Bean plant
- [ ] Drop into Dampé’s grave
- [ ] Complete Dampé’s race
- [ ] Open the chest and obtain the Hookshot
- [ ] Continue through the open doorway
- [ ] Play the Song of Time to move the blue block
- [ ] Exit into the Windmill
- [ ] Use the spinning platform to reach Heart Piece #19
- [ ] Stop after a progress audit with the Hookshot
