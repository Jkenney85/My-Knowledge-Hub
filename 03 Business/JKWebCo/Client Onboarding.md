# JKWebCo Client Onboarding

> Internal workflow, discovery call guide, and copy-ready client questionnaire.

Related notes: [[JKWebCo - Overview]] · [[Services]] · [[Pricing]]

## Onboarding principles

- Keep the first conversation focused on the client's business goals, not technical jargon.
- Recommend the smallest package that meets the client's real needs.
- Do not begin work until the agreement is signed and the 50% deposit is paid through Stripe.
- Do not start the published delivery timeline until all required content and access are received.
- Use one primary client decision-maker and require consolidated written feedback.
- Confirm all scope, pricing, exclusions, and third-party costs in writing.
- Never ask a client to send passwords through ordinary email or text.
- The signed agreement controls if it conflicts with this internal checklist.

## Onboarding workflow

### 1. New inquiry

- [ ] Record the client's name, business, email, phone number, and referral source.
- [ ] Confirm that the business and project appear to be a reasonable JKWebCo fit.
- [ ] Send the initial questionnaire or schedule a free 30-minute discovery call.
- [ ] Create a client record and a private Google Drive project folder.

### 2. Discovery

- [ ] Review the client's questionnaire before the call when available.
- [ ] Identify the website's primary business goal and target customer.
- [ ] Confirm required pages, features, integrations, and content.
- [ ] Review examples of websites the client likes and dislikes.
- [ ] Determine whether this is a Starter, Business, Advanced Custom, or standalone build.
- [ ] Discuss the client's preferred monthly care plan when applicable.
- [ ] Identify third-party costs or custom work that require a separate quote.
- [ ] Confirm the desired launch date and whether it is realistic.

### 3. Scope and approval

- [ ] Write the page list and required functionality.
- [ ] Define what JKWebCo will deliver and what is excluded.
- [ ] Document the included revision allowance.
- [ ] Confirm the estimated timeline and the conditions required for it to begin.
- [ ] Name one client decision-maker.
- [ ] Send the proposal or service agreement for signature.
- [ ] Send the 50% Stripe deposit invoice.
- [ ] Do not schedule the build until the agreement and deposit are complete.

### 4. Content and access collection

- [ ] Share the client's Google Drive folder.
- [ ] Request final copy, logo files, brand guidance, photos, and media.
- [ ] Request business details, contact information, hours, service area, and social links.
- [ ] Request client-approved privacy, terms, disclaimer, and other required legal text.
- [ ] Confirm that the client owns or has permission to use all supplied material.
- [ ] Request delegated account access wherever possible.
- [ ] Use secure password sharing only when delegated access is unavailable.
- [ ] Confirm that the client owns and controls the domain account.
- [ ] Mark content and access as complete before starting the delivery clock.

### 5. Build and review

- [ ] Confirm the sitemap and primary call to action before detailed development.
- [ ] Build against the signed scope.
- [ ] Keep out-of-scope ideas in a separate change list.
- [ ] Test the website internally before sending the first review link.
- [ ] Ask the primary decision-maker for one consolidated feedback list.
- [ ] Track revision rounds against the package allowance.
- [ ] Get written approval before starting any additional billable work.
- [ ] If the client does not respond within five business days, pause and reschedule the timeline.

### 6. Pre-launch

- [ ] Receive written final design and content approval.
- [ ] Confirm the remaining 50% invoice is paid through Stripe.
- [ ] Confirm the monthly care plan and recurring Stripe payment when selected.
- [ ] Confirm the domain connection and DNS access.
- [ ] Complete the launch quality checklist below.
- [ ] Agree on the launch date and any downtime-sensitive window.

### 7. Launch and handoff

- [ ] Publish the approved website.
- [ ] Verify the live domain and SSL certificate.
- [ ] Retest all forms, links, integrations, and analytics on the live site.
- [ ] Send the client a launch confirmation.
- [ ] Hold the included 30-minute post-launch walkthrough.
- [ ] Explain how to request updates and what the selected care plan covers.
- [ ] Confirm where the client can find their website and project files.

### 8. Follow-up

- [ ] Check the site and lead forms after launch.
- [ ] Request a testimonial after the client has had time to use the website.
- [ ] Confirm written portfolio permission before publishing a case study or showcase.
- [ ] Schedule recurring reporting or strategy reviews required by the care plan.
- [ ] Record lessons, actual build hours, revision time, and scope changes for pricing review.

## Copy-ready initial client questionnaire

### Email introduction

**Subject:** JKWebCo website project questions

Please send your answers by email. Once we decide to move forward, I will create a private Google Drive folder for your website copy, logo, photos, and other project files.

Thanks,  
James  
JKWebCo — Your Website, Handled.  
[jkwebco.com](https://jkwebco.com)

### Business and contact information

1. What is the legal business name and the name customers should see on the website?
2. Who will be the primary contact and final decision-maker for this project?
3. What are the best email address and phone number for that person?
4. What is the business address, service area, and normal business hours?
5. What products or services does the business provide?
6. How long has the business been operating?
7. What makes the business different from its competitors?
8. Who is the ideal customer?

### Website goals

9. What is the main reason you need a new website?
10. What is the single most important action visitors should take?
11. What other actions should visitors be able to take?
12. How will you know the new website is successful?
13. Is there a specific launch date or business event driving the timeline?
14. Do you currently have a website? If so, what is the address?
15. What works well on the current website?
16. What is not working or needs to change?

### Pages and content

17. Which pages do you expect the website to need? Common examples include Home, About, Services, Gallery or Portfolio, FAQ, Contact, and Privacy.
18. Do you already have final written copy for each page?
19. Do you have a finished logo and any brand colors or fonts that must be used?
20. Do you have high-quality photos, videos, testimonials, certifications, or other media?
21. Are there existing brochures, menus, service lists, or documents that can help explain the business?
22. Are you willing to provide all final content before the build timeline begins?
23. Are there any words, claims, images, or topics that must be included or avoided?

JKWebCo does not provide copywriting, logo design, or branding. The client supplies final text and brand assets. JKWebCo can quote stock-image sourcing when needed.

### Design direction

24. How should the website feel to a visitor? For example: modern, welcoming, premium, bold, simple, traditional, or playful.
25. List two or three websites you like and explain what you like about them.
26. List any websites or design styles you dislike and explain why.
27. Are there accessibility, language, or usability needs JKWebCo should know about?
28. Will anyone besides the primary decision-maker provide design feedback?

### Features and integrations

29. Does the website need a contact, quote, intake, application, or other form?
30. Does it need appointment booking or scheduling?
31. Does it need a blog, news section, gallery, or portfolio?
32. Does it need email-marketing, CRM, payment, map, review, chat, or social-media integrations?
33. Does it need customer accounts, protected content, a database, or other custom functionality?
34. Are you considering online sales or e-commerce now or later?
35. Which existing business tools must connect to the website?

### Search and marketing

36. What cities or service areas should the website target?
37. What would potential customers search for to find the business?
38. Does the business already have a Google Business Profile?
39. Does the business use Google Analytics, Search Console, advertising, or another reporting platform?
40. Which social profiles should be linked?
41. Are there existing reviews or testimonials the client has permission to display?

### Domain, ownership, and access

42. Do you already own the desired domain name?
43. Which company is the domain registered with?
44. Who controls the domain account and its billing?
45. Will you invite JKWebCo as an account user where possible?
46. Are there existing website files, analytics accounts, email services, or integrations that must be preserved?

The client should purchase and own the domain. JKWebCo will help connect and configure it. Access should be delegated through account invitations or shared securely, never through ordinary email or text.

### Package and service interest

47. Which build option seems closest to your needs: Starter, Business, Advanced Custom, or standalone custom build?
48. If you want JKWebCo to host and manage the site, which monthly care plan interests you: Basic, Standard, or Priority?
49. Are you interested in any add-ons such as extra pages, stock-image sourcing, Google Business Profile help, enhanced local SEO, extra integrations, or e-commerce?
50. Is there anything else that could affect the project's scope, cost, or timeline?

Package interest is not final. JKWebCo will confirm the correct scope and price after discovery.

### Permissions

51. May JKWebCo display the completed website in its portfolio after launch?
52. May JKWebCo contact you for a testimonial after the project is complete?

## Conditional follow-up questions

Use only the sections that apply to the project.

### Lead-generation or quote-request website

- What information must a potential lead submit?
- Which fields are required, and which should be optional?
- Where should lead notifications be sent?
- What should happen after someone submits the form?
- Does the client need consent language or qualification questions?
- Will leads need to enter a CRM, spreadsheet, or email-marketing system?

### Booking or appointment website

- What appointment types can customers book?
- Should customers choose a staff member, artist, contractor, or location?
- How long is each appointment type?
- Are deposits, full payments, or cards on file required?
- What are the cancellation, rescheduling, and no-show rules?
- Which calendar or booking platform should be used?
- What intake questions, waivers, or consent steps are required?
- Who receives booking notifications?
- Are reminder emails or texts required?

### Blog, gallery, or portfolio

- Who will supply and approve each item?
- What categories or filters are needed?
- Does each item need its own page?
- Who will publish new content after launch?
- Are captions, credits, permissions, or before-and-after disclaimers required?

### E-commerce

- What products or services will be sold?
- How many products and variations are expected at launch?
- Are the products physical, digital, subscription-based, or service-based?
- Which payment processor should be used?
- How will shipping, pickup, tax, returns, and inventory work?
- Who will supply product titles, descriptions, prices, images, and policies?
- Are age restrictions, regulated products, or special compliance requirements involved?
- Which order and inventory tools must be integrated?

E-commerce is not a core launch service for JKWebCo and must be accepted and custom-quoted after discovery.

## 30-minute discovery call guide

### Minutes 0–5: Context

- Thank the client and confirm the 30-minute agenda.
- Ask them to describe the business in their own words.
- Confirm the primary contact and decision-maker.

### Minutes 5–15: Goals and customers

- What problem should the website solve for the business?
- Who should the site attract?
- What is the main action visitors should take?
- What is wrong with the current website or online presence?
- What would make the project a clear success?

### Minutes 15–23: Scope

- Confirm pages, content, forms, booking, integrations, and future needs.
- Review design examples and required brand materials.
- Discuss domain status, content readiness, and target launch date.
- Identify risks, unknowns, and paid third-party services.

### Minutes 23–28: Package fit

- Recommend the smallest suitable build package.
- Discuss whether the client wants a monthly care plan or a standalone build.
- Explain the deposit, revision allowance, content responsibilities, and timeline conditions.

### Minutes 28–30: Next step

- Summarize the agreed direction.
- Name any unanswered questions.
- Explain when the client will receive the proposal and agreement.
- Confirm that work begins only after signature, deposit, content, and required access.

├── 03 Logo and Brand Assets/

## Launch quality checklist

### Content and design

- [ ] All approved pages and sections are present.
- [ ] Names, phone numbers, addresses, hours, prices, and service areas are accurate.
- [ ] Spelling, grammar, headings, and formatting have been reviewed.
- [ ] Images display correctly and have appropriate permission.
- [ ] The primary call to action is clear on key pages.
- [ ] Client-supplied legal and policy pages are published where required.

### Functionality

- [ ] Navigation, buttons, links, downloads, and social links work.
- [ ] Forms submit successfully and notifications reach the correct recipient.
- [ ] Booking, CRM, email, map, payment, and other integrations work.
- [ ] Confirmation messages and error states are understandable.
- [ ] The site works on common phone, tablet, and desktop sizes.

### Search, analytics, and technical setup

- [ ] Page titles and descriptions are set.
- [ ] Heading structure and image alternative text are reviewed.
- [ ] Favicon and social-sharing image are configured when supplied.
- [ ] Analytics and other approved tracking are connected.
- [ ] Domain and SSL work correctly.
- [ ] Both the primary domain and common domain variation redirect correctly when applicable.
- [ ] Performance and obvious accessibility issues are checked.

### Approval and billing

- [ ] Primary decision-maker gave written launch approval.
- [ ] Remaining build balance is paid.
- [ ] Monthly Stripe subscription is active when applicable.
- [ ] Third-party accounts and costs are documented.
- [ ] The client has received the launch and support instructions.

## Client delay and inactive-project policy

- Clients have five business days to provide requested content, feedback, or approval.
- After five business days, the delivery timeline may pause and be rescheduled based on JKWebCo availability.
- A project is considered inactive after 30 calendar days without a client response.
- Restarting an inactive project may require a new schedule, restart fee, or revised quote.
- The 50% deposit becomes non-refundable once work begins, subject to the signed agreement.

## Security and compliance boundaries

- Use delegated account access instead of shared login credentials whenever possible.
- Share unavoidable credentials through a secure password manager, never ordinary email or text.
- Do not store passwords, payment card information, or sensitive personal data in the Google Drive project folder.
- Clients are responsible for supplying privacy policies, terms, disclaimers, accessibility requirements, and other legal or regulated content.
- JKWebCo can place client-approved legal text but does not provide legal advice.
- Confirm permission to use all client-supplied copy, logos, photos, testimonials, and other assets.

## Post-launch walkthrough agenda

- Review the live website and primary customer journey.
- Demonstrate forms, booking, or other major functions.
- Show the client where the domain and key third-party accounts are managed.
- Explain how to submit update and support requests.
- Review included monthly update time, response targets, non-rollover, and additional-work approval.
- Confirm the client's care plan and billing date.
- Explain ownership, file access, and the cancellation handoff process.
- Answer final operational questions.
