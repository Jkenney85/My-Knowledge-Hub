# JKWebCo

> **Your Website, Handled.**

**Website:** [jkwebco.com](https://jkwebco.com)  
**Home market:** Blue Springs, Missouri  
**Launch strategy:** Serve Blue Springs and the Kansas City area first, then expand to remote clients.

## Business overview

JKWebCo builds useful, professional websites for small and medium-sized businesses that do not have the time or technical knowledge to create and manage a meaningful online presence.

The business combines an affordable fixed website build fee with optional ongoing hosting, maintenance, updates, and tiered support. Websites managed by JKWebCo will primarily be hosted on Lovable. Clients get one dependable point of contact instead of having to coordinate a designer, developer, hosting provider, and support service.

## Mission

Help small and medium-sized business owners get a website that supports their business without requiring them to become website experts.

## Ideal clients

- Small and medium-sized businesses that lack the time or technical expertise to build and maintain a website.
- New businesses that need a professional online presence quickly.
- Existing businesses with an outdated, ineffective, or difficult-to-manage website.
- Local service businesses that need lead forms, booking tools, portfolios, or business integrations.
- Initial opportunities include tattoo studios and contractors, but JKWebCo is not limited to a single industry.

## Core value proposition

- Affordable upfront pricing.
- Personal, local service.
- Fast and clearly defined delivery timelines.
- A done-for-you process from build through launch.
- Hosting, maintenance, updates, and support from one contact.
- Tiered care plans that let clients choose the support level they need.
- Client ownership of the domain, website files, and content after all invoices are paid.

## What JKWebCo offers

- Informational and brochure websites.
- Service-business and lead-generation websites.
- Booking and appointment websites.
- Blogs and portfolio websites.
- Websites with business-tool integrations.

## Business model

JKWebCo has two connected revenue streams:

1. Fixed-price website packages based on project complexity.
2. Recurring monthly service plans for hosting, maintenance, updates, and support.

The published fixed build prices are available when a client also selects a monthly service plan. A client may purchase a standalone build, but it will be custom-quoted at a higher price based on the complete scope.

## Client experience

1. Discovery and package selection.
2. Written scope, timeline, and payment schedule.
3. 50% build deposit.
4. Client supplies final copy, logo, brand assets, and photos.
5. JKWebCo designs and builds the website.
6. Client reviews the site during the included revision rounds.
7. Remaining 50% is paid before launch.
8. The site launches and monthly service billing begins, when selected.

## Operating principles

- Keep pricing and scope clear before work begins.
- Get approval before performing billable work beyond the included scope.
- Do not let unused monthly update time roll over.
- Keep domains in the client's name and under the client's ownership.
- Use email or a website request form for normal support requests.
- Reserve phone and text support for urgent issues.
- Begin with month-to-month service and consider minimum service terms after the business is established.

## Early priorities

- Use existing contractor relationships to find early clients.
- Develop a repeatable discovery, content-collection, build, and launch process.
- Collect testimonials and measurable client outcomes.
