# JKWebCo Services

## Website build packages

### Starter website

For a small business that needs a focused, professional online presence.

- Up to 3 pages.
- Contact form.
- Mobile-responsive design.
- Essential on-page setup and website basics.
- Domain and SSL connection assistance.
- 1 revision round.
- Estimated timeline: 1–2 weeks after the deposit and all required content are received.

### Business website

For an established or growing business that needs more content, visibility, and functionality.

- 5–8 pages.
- Everything in the Starter scope.
- Enhanced foundational SEO.
- Analytics setup.
- Blog or portfolio capability.
- 1 business integration, such as booking, email marketing, or a CRM.
- 2 revision rounds.
- Estimated timeline: 2–4 weeks after the deposit and all required content are received.

Projects that fall between the published page ranges will be placed or quoted according to their actual complexity.

### Advanced Custom website

For businesses that need a larger website, complex workflows, or custom functionality.

- 9 or more pages.
- Multiple third-party integrations.
- Advanced forms or booking workflows.
- Custom functionality.
- Project-specific revision allowance.
- Project-specific timeline established after discovery.
- E-commerce may be considered and custom-quoted when it is a good fit.

## Monthly website care

### Basic care

- Lovable hosting management, including normal platform usage.
- Domain and SSL assistance.
- Uptime and site-health monitoring.
- Basic website maintenance.
- Up to 30 minutes of minor content updates per month.
- Standard response within 3–5 business days.

### Standard care

- Everything in Basic care.
- Up to 2 hours of content updates per month.
- Monthly analytics reporting.
- Basic ongoing SEO checks.
- Standard response within 1–2 business days.

### Priority care

- Everything in Standard care.
- Up to 4 hours of updates per month.
- Advanced SEO and performance monitoring.
- Same-day or next-business-day priority response.
- Quarterly website strategy review.

Response times describe when JKWebCo will acknowledge and begin evaluating a request. Completion time depends on the request's size, complexity, required client input, and third-party dependencies.

## Optional add-ons

- Stock-image research and sourcing.
- Additional website pages.
- Additional forms, booking tools, or business integrations.
- Google Business Profile setup or optimization.
- Enhanced local SEO work.
- Custom e-commerce planning and implementation.
- Migration or transfer assistance.

Add-ons are quoted separately. Paid third-party tools, licenses, unusually high Lovable Cloud usage, and other external service costs require client approval and are billed separately.

## Client responsibilities

Clients must provide:

- Final written website copy.
- Logo and brand assets.
- Photos and other required media.
- Accurate business, contact, service, and legal information.
- Timely feedback and approvals.
- Access to the domain and any services being integrated.

JKWebCo does not offer copywriting, logo design, or branding services. Work does not enter the published delivery window until the deposit and all required content are received.

## Support and update requests

- Normal requests should be sent by email or through the JKWebCo website request form.
- Phone or text is reserved for urgent issues.
- Work beyond a plan's included time requires approval before it begins.
- Included monthly update time expires at the end of each billing month and does not roll over.

## Ownership and offboarding

- The client purchases and owns their domain.
- Once all invoices are paid, the client owns their website files and supplied content.
- Clients may receive their website files when service ends.
- Migration, export, or transfer assistance is billed at the standard hourly rate.
- Monthly service requires 30 days' written cancellation notice and continues through the paid billing period.
