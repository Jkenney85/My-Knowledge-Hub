# JKWebCo Pricing

> **Your Website, Handled.**

## Website build packages

The fixed prices below are available when the client also selects a JKWebCo monthly service plan.

| Package | Build price | Scope | Revisions | Estimated timeline |
|---|---:|---|---:|---|
| Starter | **$750** | Up to 3 pages, contact form, mobile-responsive design, and essential website basics | 1 round | 1–2 weeks |
| Business | **$1,500** | 5–8 pages, enhanced foundational SEO, analytics, blog or portfolio, and 1 business integration | 2 rounds | 2–4 weeks |
| Advanced Custom | **Starting at $3,000** | 9+ pages, multiple integrations, advanced workflows, or custom functionality | Defined per project | Defined per project |

Projects between the published page ranges are priced according to complexity. E-commerce is selectively offered and always custom-quoted.

### Standalone website builds

Clients are not required to purchase ongoing service. A standalone website build is custom-quoted at a higher price because it does not include the recurring service relationship tied to the published package pricing.

### Build payment schedule

- 50% deposit to schedule and begin the project.
- Remaining 50% due before launch or final file delivery.
- Monthly service billing begins on the website's launch date.
- Work outside the approved build scope requires a written change approval and may change the price or timeline.

## Monthly service plans

| Plan | Monthly price | Included update time | Response target | Key additions |
|---|---:|---:|---|---|
| Basic | **$99/month** | 30 minutes | 3–5 business days | Lovable hosting management, normal platform usage, domain/SSL assistance, monitoring, and basic maintenance |
| Standard | **$249/month** | 2 hours | 1–2 business days | Everything in Basic, monthly analytics reporting, and basic SEO checks |
| Priority | **$499/month** | 4 hours | Same day or next business day | Everything in Standard, advanced SEO/performance monitoring, and a quarterly strategy review |

Response targets cover acknowledgment and initial evaluation, not guaranteed completion of every request within that window.

## Additional work and external costs

- Work beyond included monthly time: **$100 per hour**.
- Additional work is billed in 30-minute increments.
- Client approval is required before billable additional work begins.
- Unused monthly time expires and does not roll over.
- Normal Lovable hosting usage is included in the monthly plan.
- Unusually high Lovable Cloud usage, paid integrations, premium tools, licenses, and other third-party charges are billed separately with client approval.

## Monthly service terms

- Plans begin month to month; there is no initial minimum service term.
- 30 days' written notice is required to cancel.
- Service continues through the current paid billing period.
- The client keeps their paid-for website files and content after cancellation.
- Migration, export, and transfer help is billed at the standard hourly rate.

## Optional add-ons

The following are quoted according to scope:

- Additional pages.
- Stock-image sourcing.
- Additional integrations.
- Google Business Profile setup or optimization.
- Enhanced local SEO.
- E-commerce functionality.
- Website migration or transfer work.

Copywriting, logo design, and branding are not offered. Clients provide their final copy and brand materials.

## Pricing review notes

These are launch prices and should be reviewed after the first 3–5 completed client projects. Track actual build hours, revision time, monthly support usage, Lovable costs, add-on demand, and client objections before changing the packages.
