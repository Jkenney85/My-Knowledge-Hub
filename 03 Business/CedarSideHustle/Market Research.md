## Initial channel recommendations

1. **Facebook Marketplace:** first test channel, as originally proposed by the user. Use finished prototype photos, measured dimensions, and a clear pickup/delivery policy.
2. **Local neighborhood or gardening buy/sell groups:** possible supplementary channel where the group permits business listings; verify each group's rules before posting.
3. **OfferUp:** possible secondary local listing test. A Kansas City cedar-planter listing appeared in the initial research; this is evidence of a local listing, not evidence of buyer demand or completed sales.
4. **Maker markets:** consider after online sales establish demand and margins. Booth fees, displays, transport, and attendance time add costs.

These are recommendations for future testing. No listings, vendor applications, or outreach have been made.

### Preliminary cost scenario

Only the picket price is confirmed. The allowance below is a planning assumption, not a supplier quote or measured production cost.

| Item | Amount per planter |
| --- | --- |
| Five cedar pickets before purchase tax | $17.40 confirmed |
| Allowance for glue, fasteners, liner, sanding consumables, and material purchase tax | $7.60–$12.60 assumed |
| Total material/consumable planning budget | $25–$30 assumed |
| $65 price less that planning budget | $35–$40 available for labor, overhead, and profit before any uncharged travel or selling fees |

Illustration only: if hands-on production takes one hour and labor is valued at $25/hour, $10–$15 remains after the assumed materials and labor, before other overhead, sales/admin time, selling fees, and uncharged travel. Neither build time nor the user's desired hourly return is established.

Recalculate after the prototype. Review both per-unit contribution and total time spent on messages, purchasing, building, and handoff before discounting or increasing stock. Allocate glue, liner, and fastener costs by actual usage; the first purchase of whole bottles, rolls, and boxes may exceed the per-unit allowance and leave stock for later builds.

## Launch inventory test

The square-planter cost and price examples above apply to the original product. The additional [[03 Business/CedarSideHustle/14x28x24 Cedar Planter|six-leg rectangular prototype]] uses eight pickets, **$27.84 in lumber before tax**; its other costs and selling price require a separate calculation.

- User preference: build a few initially and decide from demand whether to keep stock or build to order.
- Suggested test: one prototype, then two saleable units once fit, drainage, and support have been checked.
- Suggested lumber allocation: five pickets for the prototype, then ten for the two saleable units; **15 pickets total, $52.20 before tax** at the supplied price.
- Publish photos of the actual completed product. For later orders, agree on a lead time based on measured production capacity.
- Track how often buyers require immediate availability, how long ready units sit, and how quickly replacements can be completed.
- If sales are slow, review photos, listing clarity, local comparisons, and feedback before making more stock or automatically cutting the price.

- Feedback on appearance, size, lining, and optional matching pairs.
