# Operations

Back to [[Waterfield Detailing - Overview|Waterfield Detailing]]

## Weekly capacity
- **Weeknights:** 1 car per evening.
- **Saturday and Sunday:** 2–3 cars per day.
- Total: roughly 9–11 cars per week at full pace.

**Daily limits:** weeknights 1 car per evening (Full Details allowed). Weekends up to 3 cars per day, max 2 Full Details. Jobs may run past normal hours. A weeknight Full Detail means a late night, so pick the car up right at 4:30.

## Seasonal plan
- **Daylight:** sunset is around 7pm now and drops to roughly 5:15pm after the time change on Nov 1. Good work lights are a launch requirement for evening jobs.
- **Cold weather:** products and water behave poorly below about 40°F, and wet surfaces can freeze. Options:
  - Garage work, if available
  - Interior-only jobs on cold days
  - Weekend-only hours in winter
- **Spring:** biggest demand (pollen, road salt cleanup). Good time to run a big push.

## Job workflow
1. **Booking:** customer picks a service and time; confirm by text.
2. **Pickup:** walkaround photos (all panels, wheels, interior), note odometer and fuel, collect keys, confirm valuables are removed.
3. **Detail:** follow the step-by-step checklist in [[Process & Products]].
4. **Quality check:** walk the car with a flashlight, check glass and jambs.
5. **After photos.**
6. **Drop-off:** walk around the car with the customer and collect the balance **before handing back the keys**. If nobody's home, keep the car until it can be handed off (unless it's paid in full or they arranged it beforehand).
   - Unpaid balance: reminder texts, then a $15 late fee after 7 days, and no new bookings until paid. See [[Terms & Policies]].
7. **Follow-up:** thank-you text, ask for a Google review, offer the Maintenance Detail.

## Marketing (neighborhood-first)
- Neighborhood Facebook group and Nextdoor posts with before/after photos.
- Launch offer: first 10 Full Details discounted in exchange for photos and reviews.
- Referral program: referrer and new customer both get $10 off their next 3 services. See [[Services & Pricing#Referral program]].
- Door hangers or flyers, if the HOA allows them.
- Google Business Profile and a simple one-page site with a booking form.
- No yard signs: the city doesn't allow business signage in residential neighborhoods.

## Path to full-time mobile
- Build a recurring client base with Maintenance Details.
- Track actual job times and profit per car.
- Save toward a mobile rig (water tank, generator or battery, trailer or van).
- Add higher-end services: clay bar, paint correction, ceramic coating.
