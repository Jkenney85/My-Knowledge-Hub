## Waterfield Detailing: Service Terms
*Last updated: [DATE]*

By booking a service with Waterfield Detailing ("we," "us"), you agree to these terms.

### 1. Booking and deposits
- We ask for a **deposit of 30%** of your service price when you book. It goes toward your final bill.
- Deposits can be paid online or by Venmo. We strongly encourage one, because it secures your spot.

### 2. Cancellations and rescheduling
- **Cancel or reschedule 48 hours or more before** your appointment: full deposit refund, or we move it to your new date.
- **Cancel or reschedule less than 48 hours before**, or no-show: the deposit is non-refundable.
- **No-show** means your car and keys aren't available within 15 minutes of the scheduled pickup time, or the car isn't dropped off as scheduled.
- **If we cancel** (weather, illness or anything else), you choose a full refund or a new date.

### 3. Pricing and vehicle condition
- Prices are based on vehicle size and normal condition.
- **Heavy pet hair, sand, stains, smoke, or excessive dirt** may cost extra. We'll always contact you and get your OK before doing extra work. If you decline, we'll do the booked service only.
- We may decline or separately quote vehicles with **biohazards** (vomit, mold, bodily fluids, needles).

### 4. Payment
- **Your remaining balance is due at drop-off, before your keys are returned.** We accept card, Venmo and cash.
- If you're not home at drop-off, we'll hold your car until we can hand it off, unless you've paid in full or arranged it with us beforehand.
- **Late payment:** if a balance goes unpaid, we'll send reminders. Balances unpaid after **7 days** have a **$15 late fee**. We can't accept new bookings, or apply referral credits, until your balance is paid.

### 5. Pickup and drop-off
- By booking pickup and drop-off, you authorize us to drive your vehicle between your home and our location.
- Your vehicle must be registered, insured, and safe to drive. We may decline a vehicle we don't think is safe to drive.
- At pickup, we take walkaround photos and note mileage and fuel level to record your vehicle's condition.

### 6. Personal belongings
- Please remove valuables and personal items before your appointment.
- We aren't responsible for items left in the vehicle. Anything we find will be bagged and returned to you.

### 7. Satisfaction and re-dos
- We'll walk around the car with you at drop-off.
- If something isn't right, tell us at the walkthrough or **within 24 hours**. We'll re-do that area **free of charge**.
- We don't offer refunds for completed services. We'll make it right with a re-do instead.

### 8. What we can't guarantee
Detailing makes a big difference, but some things can't be fully fixed, including:
- Permanent or set-in stains, dye transfer, and sun fading
- Scratches, chips, swirl marks, and paint defects that were already there. Our services clean and protect; they don't correct paint.
- Odors that return from their source (e.g. smoke, mildew, or spills inside the seat foam or HVAC system)
- Worn, cracked or torn interior surfaces
- Failing clear coat, oxidation, or previous poor repairs

### 9. Damage
- Please report any damage you believe we caused **within 24 hours** of drop-off. We'll compare it to our pickup photos.
- We aren't responsible for pre-existing damage, or for the failure of parts that were already worn or loose: loose trim, aftermarket accessories, failing paint, or electronics.

### 10. Photos
- We take before and after photos of every job for our records.
- We'll only post photos of your vehicle online with your permission. License plates and addresses are never shown.

### 11. Referral program
- When you refer a new customer, you both get **$10 off your next 3 services**, once their first service is completed and paid.
- One $10 credit per service. Each extra referral adds 3 more credits. Credits don't expire and have no cash value.
- Referral credits can't be combined with launch or promotional offers.

### 12. Text messages
- If you agree to texts, we'll send appointment confirmations, reminders, pickup and drop-off updates, and follow-ups.
- Message frequency varies. Msg & data rates may apply. Reply **STOP** to opt out or **HELP** for help.
- We don't sell your number or send marketing texts from other businesses.

### 13. Changes
We may update these terms from time to time. The version posted on our website when you book applies to your appointment.
