# Services & Pricing

Back to [[Waterfield Detailing - Overview|Waterfield Detailing]] · Based on [[Market Research]]

Positioning: at or a little under established local detailers, with free neighborhood pickup and drop-off as the differentiator.

## Launch menu

| Service | Car (sedan/coupe) | SUV / Truck | Time (est.) |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Exterior Detail** | $69 | $89 | 1.5–2 hrs |
| **Interior Detail** | $139 | $169 | 2.5–3 hrs |
| **Full Detail** (interior + exterior) | $209 | $249 | 4–5 hrs |

Large 3-row SUVs, minivans and full-size trucks: **+$25**.
Pickup and drop-off: **free** within the neighborhood.

### What's included
**Exterior Detail**
- Foam pre-wash and two-bucket hand wash
- Wheels, tires and wheel wells
- Door jambs
- Bug and tar removal
- Spray sealant
- Exterior glass
- Tire dressing

**Interior Detail**
- Full vacuum, including trunk and under seats
- Steam clean of vents, cupholders, console and high-touch surfaces
- Plastics and vinyl cleaned and dressed
- Leather cleaned and conditioned
- Stains spot-treated
- Floor mats cleaned
- Interior glass

**Full Detail**
- Everything in both

## Recurring customers
- **Maintenance Detail** (touchless exterior wash, sealant top-up, light interior): **$119 car / $139 SUV-truck**
- Only for cars that had a Full Detail in the last **30 days**, so the protection from the last detail is still there and a touchless wash is enough.
- Recurring clients are what make the business steady; push this after every Full Detail.

## Launch offer
- **First 10 Full Details: $169 car / $199 SUV-truck**
- In exchange: permission for before/after photos and an honest Google review.
- Once bookings are 2+ weeks out, stop discounting and move to standard pricing.

## Referral program
**Give $10, get $10, three times over.** When an existing customer refers a new customer, both get **$10 off their next 3 services**, worth $30 each.

How it works:
- The new customer mentions who referred them when booking.
- The new customer's credit applies to their first 3 services.
- The referrer's credits unlock once the new customer's first service is completed and paid.
- One $10 credit per service. Each extra referral adds 3 more credits to the referrer's balance.
- Credits apply to any service (Exterior, Interior, Full, Maintenance) and don't expire.
- Suggested: referral credits don't stack with the launch offer.
- Track every referral and remaining credits (e.g. a simple table: customer, referred by, credits left).

Cost to the business: $60 in total discounts per successful referral, spread across 6 services.

## Travel fees (draft, not active)
For requests from outside the neighborhood that I choose to accept. Distance is measured from home.

| Distance | Fee |
|---|---|
| Inside the neighborhood | Free |
| Up to 3 miles | $10 |
| 3–7 miles | $20 |
| Beyond 7 miles | Case by case, or decline |

Things to think through before using this:
- **Pickup logistics:** inside the neighborhood I can walk to get a car. Farther out, I need a ride back (or a bike or scooter), and that's two round trips per job. The fee should cover that time.
- **Customer drop-off** would avoid the trips, but city home-business rules may not allow customer traffic. See [[Startup Checklist]].
- **Competition:** some local mobile detailers advertise "no travel fees" across Blue Springs. Keep fees modest.
- Once I go fully mobile, travel pricing gets replaced by a proper service-area map.

## Sanity check: what this pays
Rough gross per hour, before ~$10–15 in supplies per car:
- Exterior: $69 ÷ ~1.75 hrs ≈ **$39/hr**
- Interior: $139 ÷ ~2.75 hrs ≈ **$51/hr**
- Full: $209 ÷ ~4.5 hrs ≈ **$46/hr**

At the planned pace (about 5 weeknight cars + 4–6 weekend cars), a mixed week averaging ~$150/car is roughly **$1,350–1,650 gross/week** (before referral credits).

## Pricing rules
- Raise prices when consistently booked out 2+ weeks.
- Always quote heavy pet hair, sand or stains before starting. Text the customer before adding anything.
- Revisit this page after the first 20 cars using actual job times.
