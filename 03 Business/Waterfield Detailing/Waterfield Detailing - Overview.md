# Waterfield Detailing

Neighborhood car detailing business, starting small from home with a long-term goal of full-time mobile detailing.

## The model (as planned)
- **Where:** my driveway. Neighbors drop off, or I pick up and drop off (all within the neighborhood).
- **Services at launch:** Exterior Detail, Interior Detail, Full Detail. Higher-end work (clay, paint correction, ceramic) comes later.
- **Experience:** years of detailing my own and friends' cars.
- **Capacity:** 1 car per weeknight evening, 2–3 cars per day on Saturday and Sunday.
- **Pricing position:** match, or come in a little under, established local detailers.
- **Long-term:** grow into full-time mobile detailing.

> [!warning] Check before launch
> The City of Blue Springs home-based business rules say customers can't come to a home business for services, and no signage or outdoor storage of business materials is allowed. That may rule out customer drop-offs. See [[Startup Checklist]].
