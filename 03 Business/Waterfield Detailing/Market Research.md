## Takeaways
- **Full detail range:** about $249 (shop, starting price) to $350–400 (mobile).
- **Interior-only range:** about $159–275.
- **Closest direct comparison:** Atlas, a Blue Springs shop. Customers drive to them; I offer free pickup and drop-off.
- **Mobile detailers charge a premium** because they bring water and power. I'm not mobile yet, so pricing near the shop tier makes sense.
- Several competitors push **monthly maintenance plans**, so recurring pricing is standard in this market.
