### Microfiber towels (color system)
Each color only ever touches its own surfaces. A towel never moves "up" (e.g. from wheels to paint), even after washing.

## Before every job
- [ ] Review the booking: service, add-ons, vehicle size, customer notes
- [ ] Load out: towels by color (green, blue, yellow, gray + drying), full chemical bottles, charged lights, extension cords
- [ ] Walkaround photos at pickup: all 4 corners, each wheel, interior, dash (odometer and fuel)
- [ ] Note existing damage on the job record
- [ ] Remove personal items. Bag and label anything found.
- [ ] Set up the canopy and lights if needed. Park in the shade; never wash hot paint.

---

## Exterior Detail
~1.5–2 hrs · Wheels first, then top to bottom.

1. [ ] **Wheels and tires**: wheels first so dirty spray doesn't land on clean paint.
   - Tools: wheel bucket, wheel face brush, barrel brush, tire brush, wheel well brush, pressure washer
   - Products: CLEAN. Wheel & Tire Cleaner in the V+ premium sprayer
   - Clean one wheel at a time, and don't let wheel cleaner dry.
2. [ ] **Pre-rinse**: whole car, top down.
   - Tools: pressure washer (wide tip, safe distance)
3. [ ] **Foam pre-wash**
   - Tools: foam cannon, pressure washer
   - Products: CLEAN. Shampoo V2 in the foam cannon
   - Dwell ~3–5 min. Don't let it dry. Rinse top down.
4. [ ] **Bug and tar removal**: front bumper, mirrors, lower panels.
   - Products: 3D Bug Remover (handles tar too)
5. [ ] **Two-bucket contact wash**: top down, straight lines, rinse the mitt often.
   - Tools: 2 buckets with grit guards, wash mitt
   - Products: CLEAN. Shampoo V2
   - Lower panels last.
6. [ ] **Door jambs, hood and trunk edges, fuel door**
   - Tools: detailing brush, ⚫ gray towel
   - Products: CLEAN. Interior Quick Detailer V2, medium duty (1:4–1:8)
7. [ ] **Final rinse**
8. [ ] **Dry**
   - Tools: Blackline drying towels (×2), air blower for mirrors, grilles, emblems and wheels
9. [ ] **Spray sealant**: paint panel by panel, then wheels.
   - Products: Chem-X Snake Oil on paint; CLEAN. V2 Ceramic Spray Sealant on wheels
   - Tools: 🟢 green towels
10. [ ] **Exterior glass**: including top edges of windows.
    - Products: Invisible Glass or Sprayway Glass Cleaner · Tools: 🔵 blue glass towels
11. [ ] **Tire dressing**: wipe any overspray off the wheels.
    - Products: CLEAN. Tire Dressing V2 · Tools: tire applicator
12. [ ] **Final inspection**: walk around with a flashlight. Check for water drips, streaks and missed spots.

---

## Interior Detail
~2.5–3 hrs · Top to bottom, dry work before wet work.

1. [ ] **Remove** floor mats and trash. Bag personal items.
2. [ ] **Blow out / dry brush**: vents, seams, seat rails, console.
   - Tools: air blower, soft detailing brushes
3. [ ] **Vacuum**: headliner edges → seats → console → carpet → under seats → trunk.
   - Tools: shop vac + crevice and brush attachments
4. [ ] **Steam clean**: vents, cupholders, console, door pockets, high-touch surfaces.
   - Tools: Wagner 905e steam cleaner, 🟡 yellow towels
5. [ ] **Plastics and vinyl**: dash, doors, console, pillars.
   - Products: CLEAN. Interior Quick Detailer V2. Cleans and protects in one step.
     - Heavy grime: neat or 1:1 · Normal: 1:4–1:8 · Light wipe-down: 1:10–1:12
   - Tools: detailing brushes, 🟡 yellow towels
6. [ ] **Leather**: clean, then condition.
   - Products: Chemical Guys Leather Cleaner, then Chemical Guys Leather Conditioner · Tools: leather brush, applicator pad
7. [ ] **Stain spot-treatment**: cloth seats and carpet.
   - Products: Chemical Guys Foaming Citrus · Tools: spot cleaner, upholstery brush
8. [ ] **Floor mats**
   - Rubber: CLEAN. Interior Quick Detailer V2 (neat–1:1) + brush, rinse, dry
   - Carpet: vacuum, Chemical Guys Foaming Citrus, spot cleaner
9. [ ] **Protection check**: no separate dressing; protection comes from the Interior Quick Detailer V2 in step 5. Wipe any high spots or streaks. Nothing slippery on the pedals or steering wheel.
10. [ ] **Interior glass**: windshield, rear glass, mirrors, screens (screen-safe product).
    - Products: Invisible Glass or Sprayway Glass Cleaner · Tools: 🔵 blue glass towels
11. [ ] **Final vacuum and reinstall mats**
12. [ ] **Final inspection**: flashlight check of seams, cupholders and door pockets. Seats and mirrors back where they were.

---

## Full Detail
~4–5 hrs · Exterior and Interior together, in this order:
1. [ ] **Exterior steps 1–8** (wheels through drying)
2. [ ] **Interior steps 1–12**, while the paint finishes drying
3. [ ] **Exterior steps 9–12** (sealant, glass, tire dressing, inspection)
4. [ ] Final walkaround of the whole car

---

## Maintenance Detail
Quick upkeep for cars that had a Full Detail in the last **30 days**. Uses a **touchless wash**, not a contact wash, since the paint is still protected from the last detail.
1. [ ] **Wheels and tires** (Exterior step 1)
   - Products: CLEAN. Wheel & Tire Cleaner in the V+ premium sprayer
2. [ ] **Touchless wash**: Chem-X Stars + Stripes kit
   - Step 1 **Stars** (low pH): apply bottom to top, dwell 30–60 sec
   - Step 2 **Stripes** (high pH): apply over Stars, bottom to top, dwell 30–60 sec
   - Rinse thoroughly, starting at the bottom
   - Never let either product dry. In sun or heat, work in smaller sections and rinse faster.
   - Tools: foam cannon or injector, pressure washer
3. [ ] **Dry** (Exterior step 8)
4. [ ] **Sealant top-up**: Chem-X Snake Oil on paint; CLEAN. V2 Ceramic Spray Sealant on wheels
5. [ ] **Glass** inside and out
6. [ ] **Tire dressing**: CLEAN. Tire Dressing V2
7. [ ] **Interior**: vacuum, then wipe down high-touch surfaces with CLEAN. Interior Quick Detailer V2, light duty (1:10–1:12)
8. [ ] **Final inspection**

## Add-ons
### Pet hair removal
- Tools: pet hair rubber brush / stone (TBD), shop vac, air blower
- Work in short strokes toward the vacuum. Do this before the main vacuum.

### Seat and carpet shampoo (extraction)
- Tools: spot cleaner / extractor, upholstery brush
- Products: Chemical Guys Foaming Citrus
- Don't over-wet. Leave windows cracked or run a fan so it dries, and tell the customer if seats are still damp.

### Odor treatment
1. [ ] **Find and remove the source** first: spills, trash under seats, pet accidents, the cabin air filter area.
2. [ ] **Enzyme cleaner** on organic sources (pet urine, vomit, milk, food). Let it dwell per label, then extract with the spot cleaner. (Product to try.)
3. [ ] **Chlorine dioxide treatment** for odors that remain (smoke, pet, mildew). (Product to try, e.g. the Detail King ClO₂ cup.)
   - Car must be **empty of people and pets** during treatment. Windows up, AC on recirculate if the product says to.
   - Standard cups take ~3–6 hrs, so run it overnight after an evening job. Quick-release versions take ~20–30 min.
   - **Air the car out thoroughly** before driving it or returning it.
4. [ ] Note on the job record what the source was and what was used.
- Skip ozone generators for now. They work, but need more equipment and care, and repeated use can damage rubber and interior materials.
