🥚 Beef, Egg & Rice Meal Prep Bowls

**Makes 5 servings** · ~700 calories · ~55g protein per bowl (rough estimate)

### **Ingredients**
*   2 lbs lean ground beef (93/7)
*   10 eggs
*   1 ¼ cups dry rice (≈ 3 ¾ cups cooked)
*   1 ½ packets taco seasoning (≈ 3 tbsp)
*   1 ¼ cups shredded sharp cheddar (¼ cup per bowl)

---

### **Instructions**

**1. Start the Rice**
Cook the rice in a rice cooker or on the stovetop.

**2. Brown the Beef**
Brown the ground beef in a large skillet, breaking it up as it cooks. Drain the fat.

**3. Season**
Add the taco seasoning and the water called for on the packets (1 ½× one packet's amount). Simmer until thickened.

**4. Scramble the Eggs**
Scramble the eggs, pulling them off the heat while still slightly wet. They finish cooking when reheated and won't turn rubbery.

**5. Assemble**
*   Divide into 5 containers: ~¾ cup rice, then the meat and eggs.
*   Let cool, then sprinkle **¼ cup cheddar** on each before lidding. The cheese melts when reheated.

---

### **Storage & Reheating**
*   **Fridge:** Good for 4 days. Freeze the 5th bowl and move it to the fridge the night before.
*   **Reheat:** Microwave **2–2 ½ minutes**. Add a splash of water over the rice to keep it from drying out.
*   **Note:** Cut the cheese to 3 tbsp per bowl to save ~50 calories.
