🍔 Big Mac Bowls

**Makes 5 servings** · ~700 calories · ~55g protein per bowl (rough estimate)

### **Ingredients**
**The Bowls**
*   2 lbs lean ground beef (93/7)
*   1 ½ cups dry rice
*   1 ¼ cups shredded sharp cheddar (¼ cup per bowl)
*   2 Roma tomatoes, diced (≈ 1 cup)
*   3–4 pickles, chopped
*   Shredded lettuce (1 bag or head)

**Beef Seasoning**
*   Salt & pepper
*   Garlic powder
*   Splash of Worcestershire sauce
*   *Optional:* Onion powder

**Big Mac Sauce**
*   1 ¼ cups nonfat Greek yogurt
*   ⅓ cup light mayo
*   ⅓ cup ketchup
*   2 ½ tbsp mustard
*   1 ½ tbsp pickle juice

---

### **Instructions**

**1. Start the Rice**
Cook the rice in a rice cooker or on the stovetop.

**2. Brown the Beef**
Brown the ground beef in a large skillet, breaking it up as it cooks. Drain the fat, then season with salt, pepper, garlic powder, and Worcestershire.

**3. Make the Sauce**
Whisk together the Greek yogurt, mayo, ketchup, mustard, and pickle juice. Store in a jar.

**4. Assemble**
*   **Main container (reheated):** ~¾ cup rice + beef, topped with **¼ cup cheddar**.
*   **Side container (kept cold):** Tomatoes, pickles, and lettuce.
*   **Note:** Don't mix the sauce or cold toppings in before reheating. The yogurt separates and the tomatoes go mushy.

---

### **Storage & Reheating**
*   **Fridge:** Good for 4 days. Freeze the 5th main container and move it to the fridge the night before. The sauce keeps all week in the jar.
*   **Reheat:** Microwave the main container **2–2 ½ minutes** (splash of water over the rice), then add the cold toppings and sauce.
*   **Note:** Cut the rice to ½ cup per bowl to save ~70 calories.
