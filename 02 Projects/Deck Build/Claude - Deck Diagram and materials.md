All coordinates measure from the **finished deck corner (0,0)** -- the southwest corner

of the 31 ft x 21 ft decking surface. X runs along the 31-ft side, Y along the 21-ft

side. The FRAME is inset 1 1/2 in on all four sides (30'-9" x 20'-9" outside of rims)

so the 2x10 fascia finishes the deck at exactly 31 ft x 21 ft. Dimensions in the text

govern over the sketches.
