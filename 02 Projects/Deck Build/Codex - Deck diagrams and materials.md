### Foundation requirements

  

- 18 total piers, one below each post.

- Each footing uses a 22-inch round x 8-inch-thick bottom pad.

- Use a 12-inch-diameter concrete shaft above the pad.

- Bottom of footing: 38 inches below finished grade.

- Concrete pier top: 2 inches above finished grade.

- Use 5/8-inch cast-in anchors centered with a setting template.

- Do not backfill or conceal footings until the required inspection is released.

### Exact decking layout

  

- Finished surface is exactly 31'-0" x 21'-0".

- End picture-frame boards occupy X = 0-5.5 inches and X = 366.5-372 inches.

- The center breaker is centered at X = 186 inches and occupies X = 183.25-188.75 inches.

- Provide four 1/8-inch longitudinal separation gaps: end border/field and field/breaker on each side.

- Each field-board piece is 177.5 inches, or 14'-9-1/2".

- Use 43 full field courses between the two 5-1/2-inch long picture-frame borders.

- Balance the 44 cross-deck gaps to total exactly 4.5 inches. The planning pattern is 32 gaps at 3/32 inch and 12 gaps at 1/8 inch, distributed symmetrically.

- Install from both long sides toward the center using a story pole. Do not leave a narrow ripped final course.

- Predrill every fastener near a board end or knot and all picture-frame/breaker fasteners.
