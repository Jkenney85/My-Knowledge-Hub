## Workflow

1. Capture a hypothesis with [[10 Templates/Trading Research|Trading Research]].
2. Define setup rules, invalidation, and test criteria before reviewing results.
3. Record executions with [[10 Templates/Trade Review|Trade Review]].
4. Separate observed evidence from interpretations and future changes.
5. Promote stable findings into a strategy guide.

## Review prompts

- What evidence would disprove the current hypothesis?
- Are rule changes based on a meaningful sample or one memorable trade?
- Which mistakes are execution errors versus strategy errors?
- What is the next test?
