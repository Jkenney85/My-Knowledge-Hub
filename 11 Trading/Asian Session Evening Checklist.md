# Asian Session Evening Checklist

Gold (MGC), evening session. All times **Central**. Work top to bottom — if a gate fails, stop there and close the laptop.

Source model: [[Asian Session Gold ORB Model]]

---

## Before 5:00 PM — bias

- [ ] What did the **New York session** do today? Trend up, trend down, or range?
- [ ] Did NY make higher lows or lower highs into the close?
- [ ] Where did the **NY ORB** sit, and did price reject its 50%?
- [ ] Any higher-timeframe **KPL** hit today — prior day high/low, 4H 50/200 SMA?
- [ ] Was the reaction at that KPL confirmed by a **4H candle close**, or just a wick?
- [ ] **Write the two scenarios.** If price holds above ____ I'm bullish to ____. If it closes below ____ I'm bearish to ____.

> [!warning] Gate 1
> No written bullish **and** bearish scenario → no trade tonight. Not "I'll figure it out live."

---

## 5:00–5:15 PM — mark the box

- [ ] 5-minute ORB marked (5:00–5:05)
- [ ] 15-minute ORB marked (5:00–5:15)
- [ ] 50% midlines visible on both
- [ ] Note the range width — narrow range means trade the **edges**; wide range means the **midline** matters

---

## 5:15–7:00 PM — hands off

- [ ] **Not trading.** Whatever price does here is noise.
- [ ] Optional: note whether price is holding above or below the box, for context only.

> [!warning] Gate 2
> Any urge to enter before 7:00 PM is the exact mistake the model was built to fix. A break here is not a signal.

---

## 7:00 PM — volume check

- [ ] Is volume actually picking up, or still flat?
- [ ] Which side of the ORB is price working on?
- [ ] Are candles **closing** beyond a box edge, or only wicking through it?

---

## 8:00 PM — the window

- [ ] Does the ORB reaction **agree with the bias** I wrote before 5 PM?
- [ ] If they disagree: **stand down.** Do not let the box overrule the higher-timeframe read.
- [ ] Have I already missed the initial move? Good — that was information, not a loss.
- [ ] Bands: green, red, or yellow?

---

## Entry

- [ ] This is a **retest**, not the initial break
- [ ] Price is back at an ORB edge, the 50% midline, or deep into compressed (yellow) bands
- [ ] On a larger candle, roughly **50% of it is holding**
- [ ] I can see a **reaction candle** at the level — a real rejection wick, not a hover
- [ ] Micro trend break confirms: the pullback stalled and the candle flipped my way
- [ ] Entry is **close to the level**, so risk is small

> [!warning] Gate 3
> No defined stop → no entry. If I can't say the exact price where this trade is wrong, I don't have a trade.

---

## Risk

- [ ] Stop is just outside the ORB, beyond the reaction candle
- [ ] Risk in dollars is sized, written down, and acceptable
- [ ] Target is the next KPL or prior-session level — **not** a round number I invented
- [ ] Target is realistic against the **prior leg's size**. Base hit, not home run.

---

## Managing

- [ ] Moving to break-even — early, or holding? *(Decide before entry, not during. My own journal data settles this, not the video.)*
- [ ] Trailing behind pivot points as it works
- [ ] If TradingView feels laggy, factor a few seconds of slippage into the exit

---

## Stop-trading triggers

Any one of these ends the night:

- [ ] Two losers in a row
- [ ] Bias and ORB reaction contradict each other
- [ ] Past my cutoff time — a tired entry at midnight is not an edge
- [ ] Range is so tight there's no room between entry and the nearest level
- [ ] I'm entering because I've been waiting for two hours and want something to happen

---

## After

- [ ] Logged in the trade journal — entry, stop, target, outcome, and **which gate I skipped if it went wrong**
